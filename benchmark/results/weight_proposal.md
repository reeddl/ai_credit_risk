# Proposal: feature-weight scale for chip and fab segments

Generated by `python benchmark/propose_weights.py`. Nothing here is fitted to defaults;
it is fitted to six CDS quotes and placeholder risk-premium ratios.

## Proposal

Multiply every feature weight by **0.45** for chip_designer, fab_equipment (least-squares fit 0.450, rounded), and leave the segment priors where they are. Other segments keep their weights. In config this is `weight_scale = 0.45` on each segment.

Any scale from 0.25 to 0.65 puts all six names inside the band; fitted separately, the segments give chip_designer 0.43, fab_equipment 0.46.

## Why not move the priors instead

Keeping the weights and lifting the priors to the band midpoint needs chip_designer 13.9%, fab_equipment 10.1% (now 3%, 2%). It puts 6 of 6 names in the band, but the prior is the PD of an average-scoring company, so it would rate an average chip designer riskier than an average neocloud (8%). And it keeps the current spread between names within each segment. Riskiest to safest name in each segment, as a multiple: chip_designer CDS 1.5x, priors-only 1.2x, scale 0.45 1.1x; fab_equipment CDS 2.2x, priors-only 9.3x, scale 0.45 2.7x.
